Out Of Hours Setup (Do Not Disturb)

Configure the times and days when the GSM Intercom should not make calls, or redirect calls to an alternate number.

When enabled, calls will be restricted outside the defined times.

Allowed Call Days & Time

Select the days when calls are permitted and choose the time range for all selected days.

When OOH mode is enabled, calls will be redirected to this number outside permitted times. Don't fill out if you don't want this feature.
